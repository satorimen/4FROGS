# 4FROGS design
Operate mode: one decision per screen, short paths, persistent language selector and thumb-reachable primary action.
Visual authority: user's yellow-and-green relaxed frog reference. Sunflower yellow #ffcc00, forest ink #153b29, cream #fffdf3, leafy green #5a9239. Manrope variable, heavy compact display and readable body. Large organic mascot on a flat yellow home canvas, rounded green CTA, restrained outlines. Desktop uses an open two-column hero; mobile keeps the same hierarchy as a single focused screen. No restaurant catalog or marketing card grid.
Signature interaction: a sealed surprise opens only after demo delivery; clear stepped ordering and a quiet transition respecting reduced motion.
