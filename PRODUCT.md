# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
People in Israel who want a meal without choosing a restaurant or a dish.
## Product Purpose
4FROGS is a mobile-first mystery food delivery PWA. Choose savory/sweet, dietary preferences, allergies, budget, and delivery address. Keep restaurant and contents hidden until delivery.
## Brand Commitments
User-supplied frog on a saturated yellow background: yellow, leafy greens, relaxed and wry personality. Name: 4FROGS. English, Russian, Hebrew with RTL. ILS tiers 79/99/149.
## Capabilities and Constraints
This implementation is a local demonstration, not a live marketplace. No real payments, SMS, partner restaurant, address provider, or courier credentials have been supplied. Do not imply real orders, authentication, allergy capability, or delivery availability. Separate preferences and allergies. Persist local demo state, never card data. Production scope remains in docs/PRD.md.
## Open decisions
Payment/SMS/delivery providers, delivery fees, partner agreements, actual service zones and allergy capabilities.
## Product Principles
Minimum decisions. Surprise first. Safety before surprise. One-handed mobile use. No restaurant catalog.
